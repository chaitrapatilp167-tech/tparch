After you reach out

What happens next?

A simple first conversation helps us understand the project and whether we’re the right fit.

01

We listen.

We’ll learn what you hope to create, where you are in the process, and what questions are on your mind.

02

We explore.

We’ll talk through the site, timing, and scope, then suggest a clear path forward.

03

We begin.

If it feels right on both sides, we’ll outline the first phase and shape a thoughtful brief together.

A few first answers

You can start with a question.

Do I need a complete brief?

No. A location, an idea, or a question is enough to start a useful conversation.

What areas do you work in?

Our studio is in Brooklyn, and most projects are in New York and the Northeast. We consider select projects farther afield.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. If you’re nearby, mention it in your note and we can arrange a studio meeting by appointment.
